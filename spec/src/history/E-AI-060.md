The fixture used the wiki-style captured mention while the current composer
emits plain/quoted mentions. This was an integration omission, not merely bad
data: neither format had the application-owned resolver after adopting
Transcript. The fixture is retained unchanged, including its original source
text. Mira's public syntax parser supplies exclusion/wiki boundaries, and public
Transcript decorations preserve captured coordinates without a dependency patch
or rewrite.

selects the actual Welcome.md editor; returning to the JSONL tab shows its
repaired link. No native prompt was sent or review-vault file modified.
