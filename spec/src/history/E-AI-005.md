handoff orchestration. Private reasoning remains excluded and tool output
remains labeled untrusted.

summary-scope/actual-size gaps before native integration. No model or provider
invocation, automatic replay, production UI, native permission or lifecycle
change is introduced. Complete migrated stories and native application
acceptance remain under the original gate. Preserved profiles, vaults and
archive receipts are unchanged.
