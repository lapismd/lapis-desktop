## Scope and qualification

The source is copied first, with the desktop-owned draft callback and model type
adapted at the boundary. Read-only hardware is cached independently of compute
processes and is read on the existing blocking status worker. Model catalogs
provide estimates rather than measured inference speed or GPU/free-memory facts.
Unavailable installed inventory produces no installed badge. Selection, custom
entry and draft memory changes dispatch no commands.

Focused UI/native boundaries are the complete impact surface for this slice.
Full unrelated product suites, real GGUF inference/downloads, paired machines,
additional platforms and distribution are out of scope, retaining their original
M10.4 and M11 qualification gates. No provider request or model download
occurred.
